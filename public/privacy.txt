HOSTING AND THE ASSISTANT

The site and its backend run on Scaleway in Amsterdam (nl-ams), in the EU.
The assistant runs on Scaleway's Generative APIs, also in the EU. Chat
messages are sent there to produce an answer.

Scaleway keeps server logs for 7 days. These logs contain the usual request
data, including IP address.

The same request data is also sent to Better Stack, in Germany, and kept for
three days.


IN THE BROWSER

Better Stack's script runs on each page, in Germany, and keeps what it records
for 90 days. It reports:

  - JavaScript errors, and the console output around them
  - page views and loading speed
  - clicks, form submissions and scrolling
  - a session replay, which reconstructs what the page looked like and what was
    typed into it, including the chat
  - a browser fingerprint, which recognises the same browser on a later visit

Nothing from this site is used to train a model.


CHAT

The chat is optional. If you do not use it, only the request data above
applies.

If you do use it, your questions and the assistant's answers are sent to
Scaleway's Generative APIs to produce a reply, and appear in the session replay
described above.

Please don't put confidential information in the chat.


WHY THIS IS COLLECTED

This site presents professional work, and the records above are what show it is
running correctly and where it fails. The legal basis is legitimate interest
under Article 6(1)(f) GDPR.


YOUR RIGHTS

Under the GDPR you can ask for a copy of the data held about you, ask for it
to be corrected or deleted, ask for its processing to be restricted, and
object to processing based on legitimate interest.

Email the address below to make a request. For chat records, an approximate
date and time helps to find them.

You can also lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. In the
Netherlands that is the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens
(autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl).
