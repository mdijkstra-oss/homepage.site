HOSTING AND THE ASSISTANT

The site and its backend run on Scaleway in Amsterdam (nl-ams), in the EU.
The assistant runs on Scaleway's Generative APIs, also in the EU. Chat
messages are sent there to produce an answer.

Scaleway keeps server logs for 7 days. These logs contain the usual request
data, including IP address.


ANALYTICS

Page visits and clicks are sent to PostHog on its EU cloud and kept for one
year.

No cookies are set and nothing is stored on your device, so two visits from
the same person are not linked to each other.

Neither uses anything from this site to train a model.


CHAT

The chat is optional. If you do not use it, only the visit and click data
above applies.

If you do use it, the text of your questions and the assistant's answers is
sent to PostHog as well, under the same one-year retention.

Please don't put confidential information in the chat.


WHY THIS IS COLLECTED

This site presents professional work, and the assistant needs to be evaluated
to know whether it does that well. The legal basis is legitimate interest
under Article 6(1)(f) GDPR.


YOUR RIGHTS

Under the GDPR you can ask for a copy of the data held about you, ask for it
to be corrected or deleted, ask for its processing to be restricted, and
object to processing based on legitimate interest.

Email the address below to make a request. For chat records, an approximate
date and time helps to find them.

You can also lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. In the
Netherlands that is the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens
(autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl).
