THE CHAT

Your message is sent to Scaleway, which generates the answer. It is processed
in Paris and not kept: the endpoint stores no conversations, and prompts and
answers are not used to train any model. A request may be held briefly if
Scaleway is looking into abuse or an error on the service.

The questions and the answers are also stored in PostHog, on servers in
Frankfurt, so the assistant can be evaluated for accuracy. They are kept for
one year. They are not linked to a name and are not used to train any model.
Please don't put confidential information in the chat.


ANALYTICS

PostHog also records page views and click positions. Nothing is stored in the
browser, so visits cannot be linked to each other. Frankfurt, kept for one
year.


SERVER LOGS

The web server records the IP address, the page requested and the time. It
runs on Scaleway in Amsterdam, and the logs are kept for seven days.


WHY

This site presents professional work, and the assistant needs to be evaluated
to know whether it does that well. The legal basis is legitimate interest
under Article 6(1)(f) GDPR. The site works without the chat.


YOUR RIGHTS

You can request a copy of what is held about you, request its deletion, or
object to any of the above, by mail. You can also lodge a complaint with the
Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens at autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl.
