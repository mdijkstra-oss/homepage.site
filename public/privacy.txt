PRIVACY
mdijkstra.dev


Everything this site does stays in the EU.

Two companies handle it. Scaleway runs the server and generates the chat
answers. PostHog stores page views and chat turns, so the assistant can be
checked for accuracy. Both under their own privacy policies:

  https://www.scaleway.com/en/privacy-policy/
  https://posthog.com/privacy

Neither uses anything from this site to train a model. There are no cookies
and nothing else is stored on your device, so visits cannot be linked to each
other. Chat messages and analytics are kept for a year, server logs for seven
days.

Please don't put confidential information in the chat.


WHY

This site presents professional work, and the assistant needs to be evaluated
to know whether it does that well. The legal basis is legitimate interest
under Article 6(1)(f) GDPR. The site works without the chat.


YOUR RIGHTS

You can request a copy of what is held about you, request its deletion, or
object to any of the above, by mail. You can also lodge a complaint with the
Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens at autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl.
